Virtual Desktop

Useful for organizing your work and separating tasks from each other, rather than creating a new physical screen.

You can divide the work like this:

Desktop 1: Study and University

Desktop 2: Programming

Desktop 3: Cybersecurity

Desktop 4: Entertainment — YouTube and more.

Press the keyboard on the 'Win+Tab' Task View will open at the top or bottom of the screen.

Click on New Desktop.

Desktop1 and Desktop2 will appear. Like this Click on desktop2 to switch to it.

For example, open Chrome on Desktop 2

then press Win+Tab and select Desktop 1 you will see that the Chrome window from Desktop 2 does not appear on Desktop 1.

Here is an example showing both screens Desktop 1 and Desktop 2. You can switch between them quickly: use **Win + Ctrl** along with the **Right Arrow** to move right, and the **Left Arrow** to move left.
