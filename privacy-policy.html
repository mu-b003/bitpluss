Privacy Policy

Our commitments to protecting your data and advertising cookies

Information Collection and Use

At the +BIT platform, we place great importance on the privacy of our visitors. We do not directly collect any personal or sensitive data from users, as the search and filtering system works entirely locally within your browser using the built-in cache.

Cookies and Google AdSense

This website uses third-party services such as Google AdSense to display advertisements when you visit our site. Google uses cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and other websites on the internet. You can choose to disable these cookies by visiting Google’s advertising and content privacy policy.

Log Files

Like many other websites, the +BIT platform uses log files to collect general non-personally identifiable information including: browser type, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, and date and time of visits to analyze trends and manage the site.
