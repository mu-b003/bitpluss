Network

A network is a group of devices (computers, servers, printers, etc.) connected to each other to exchange data and resources

Network Types

LAN (Local Area Network): A local network that covers a small area such as a single building or office.

WLAN (Wireless LAN): The same idea as LAN but using a wireless connection (Wi-Fi).

MAN (Metropolitan Area Network): A network that covers almost an entire city, connecting several branches.

WAN (Wide Area Network): A wide network that covers large geographical areas, like the Internet itself.

Network Topologies

It is the way devices are arranged within a network, such as: Star, Bus, Ring, Mesh. The shape determines how data is transmitted and the ease of maintenance and fault detection.

Star

is a network architecture in which all devices (such as computers and printers) are connected to a single central device, usually a switch or hub . All data passes through this central device before reaching the desired device.

Features

Easy to install and manage, Faults are easy to detect and repair, The failure of one device or cable does not affect the rest of the network, It provides good performance, especially when using Switch.

Disadvantages

A failure of the central device leads to a complete network shutdown, It requires a larger quantity of cables compared to some other topologies, Higher cost due to the need for a central device.

Uses

Star topology is widely used in: Home networks, Corporate and office networks, Schools and universities, Most modern Ethernet LAN networks .
