About Us

BIT+ is a cybersecurity notes platform designed to help students, aspiring penetration testers, and security enthusiasts learn faster through clear and practical summaries.

The platform provides structured notes covering web security, Linux, Active Directory, networking fundamentals, common vulnerabilities, and security tools used in real-world environments.

Whether you are preparing for certifications, studying penetration testing, or reviewing technical concepts, BIT+ aims to provide concise and easy-to-understand learning resources.
