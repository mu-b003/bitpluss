DNS (Domain Name System)

It's what converts the name (example.com) into an IP address. But in terms of security, DNS isn't just a "phone book"—it's a goldmine of information in the Reconnaissance phase.

Important records:

A The domain connects to IPv4

AAAA The domain connects to IPv6

CNAME Alias ​​for a second domain (source of the Subdomain Takeover vulnerability)

MX Email servers

TXT Textual information (SPF, DKIM, or sometimes forgotten secrets!)

NS DNS servers responsible for the domain

Important techniques in Pentesting:

1. Subdomain Enumeration

Old spodumnes (dev, staging, test) are often the weakest protection and open a door for you to enter.

Tools: subfinder, amass, or even crt.sh(checks public SSL certificates and reveals what SDOMS know about them).

2. Subdomain Takeover:

If you find a CNAME pointing to a service (for example myapp.herokuapp.com) and the service has been deleted or cancelled, you can register the same name and take over the subdomain — a real and very dangerous vulnerability that is widespread with Bug Bounty.
