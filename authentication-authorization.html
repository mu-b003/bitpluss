Authentication & Authorization

A very important point: These are two completely different concepts and you must differentiate between them:

Authentication = "Who are you?" (Login, Identity Verification)

Authorization = "What are you allowed to do?" (Permissions after you prove your identity)

Common Authentication Problems:

Brute Force : No Rate Limiting on the login page → You can try thousands of passwords (Tools: Hydra, Burp Intruder)

If the login page does not implement rate limiting or account lockout, an attacker can perform a Brute Force attack by trying thousands of passwords automatically using tools such as Hydra or Burp Suite Intruder until the correct password is found.

Weak Password Policy : Accepting weak passwords such as123456

No MFA : No two-factor authentication

Username Enumeration : If the error message differentiates between "user not found" and "incorrect password," you can identify which emails are actually registered in the system.
